Privacy

Last updated 28 August 2026. This site is soduku.net. There is no account to create.

What stays on your device

Game progress, notes, timer, settings, and stats are saved in your browser’s localStorage. That data never leaves your computer. Clearing site data for soduku.net wipes it.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show display ads. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies is described in Google’s advertising policies.
  • You can opt out of personalised Google ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • You can opt out of interest-based ads from participating companies at aboutads.info/choices.

Ads on this site are display units in ordinary banner and rectangle slots. We do not ask you to click ads, and we do not run overlays that cover the puzzle.

Logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process standard request logs (IP address, user agent, URL) to operate the network, as described in Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
